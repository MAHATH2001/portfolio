MediQuic — AI Medical Chatbot

Reliable medical information isn't always easy to reach. MediQuic uses natural language processing to give people a friendly, conversational way to ask health questions and get useful, personalised answers back.

Watch Demo View Code
// OVERVIEW

MediQuic aims to close the gap for people who don't have easy access to personalised medical guidance. Instead of digging through search results, users can ask the chatbot directly about symptoms, nearby hospitals, therapies, diseases and prevention, or first-aid steps.

The goal is to give people a reliable, always-available first point of contact — helping them make more informed decisions about their health and easing some of the load on stretched healthcare resources. The project evolved through several modules, each adding a new layer of functionality to the chatbot.
